## 2. What Lenava Is Becoming (Post-Certificate Rebuild)

### The Core Proposition
> Help any brand find the right creators for their product — so they stop wasting budget and energy on the wrong ones.

This is **problem-led, not sector-led.** It works for:
- Ecommerce / DTC brands (physical products)
- Digital product creators
- Local service businesses (beauty clinics, hair salons, etc.)
- Any brand that uses or wants to use creator/influencer partnerships

The common thread is the problem: **misallocated creator spend.**

### What You Are NOT Doing
- Not limiting to beauty/cosmetics (that was a beachhead idea, not a hard constraint)
- Not limiting to ecommerce (creator matching works for any product or service)
- Not offering the old 4-service chatbot/automation menu anymore

## 3. The New Service Architecture

### Core Service — Creator Match Intelligence
**What it is:** You research and deliver a shortlist of the right creators for a brand — matched by audience fit, engagement quality, and growth trajectory. Not just follower count.

**Delivery model:** Services-led
1. Brand comes in (or you reach out via outreach agent)
2. Discovery conversation to understand their product, audience, and acquisition goal
3. You run the intelligence (80% automated, 20% your judgment)
4. Deliver a shortlist + rationale: "back these creators in the next 90 days, here's why"

**Entry product (to nail first):**
A focused report answering one question: *"Which creators should you back in the next 90 days and why?"*
- Not a sprawling audit
- Not a generic influencer list
- A specific, actionable shortlist with data behind it

**Growth path:** Start with one strong case study, then expand to Growth Partner retainer model.

### SaaS Layer — Market Watch
**What it is:** Creator intelligence and competitor monitoring platform. Separate from services but feeds into them.

**Two legs:**
- Competitor price tracking
- AI Visibility monitoring (how brands show up in AI search)

**Tiers:**
- Free: 1 competitor, weekly checks, 30-day history, no alerts
- Pro: €49/mo (placeholder), 10 competitors, daily checks, email alerts, AI Visibility digest

**No Stripe in MVP** — waitlist CTA only. Not live yet.

**How it connects:** Market Watch is the intelligence layer that feeds the Creator Match service. It monitors what competitors are doing with creators, tracks brand visibility, and surfaces opportunities. It's not a disconnected third product — it's the engine under the hood.

### Outreach (Phase 2 — Thin Automated Slice)
- n8n-based warm lead outreach
- You deliver replies, not just lists
- Keeps delivery automation-first (80/20 constraint)
- No heavy manual analysis
